Why

How it's scored. Hit rate counts a win as 1, a partial as ½ and a loss as 0, over judged calls only. Most jokes and unfalsifiable vibes are marked unclear or undeterminable and left out, but some were judged a win or a loss in spirit by the author, and those count. Market calls are scored on price a year after the call; trend and attention calls on the 180 days after it, adjusted for seasonality; the rest on dated, sourced events. Only evidence from after a call counts.

Verdicts come from the Unhedged tracker: automated market and search-trend evidence, pre-judgments by Claude, and the author's own rulings. The hall of fame and shame, and the doubling-down, doubling-back and butting-heads threads, are hand-picked.
